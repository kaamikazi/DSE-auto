# Official rule evidence

The evidence workspace accepts reviewed DSE, BSEC, CDBL, licensed-broker, contract-note, and fee-schedule evidence in permitted PDF, image, text, CSV, and XLSX formats. It links extracted draft claims to item-specific rule decisions.

Trading days/hours/phases, holidays/closures, ticks, price bands, suspensions, quantities, settlement, corporate actions, short selling, leverage, market orders, and expiry remain unapproved until human review. Extraction accuracy is not configuration approval.
