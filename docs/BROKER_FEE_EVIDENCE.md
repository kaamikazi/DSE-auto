# Broker fee evidence

Fee intake covers buy/sell/minimum brokerage, exchange/regulatory/tax/settlement/CDBL charges, account-specific and flat charges, and effective dates. Contract notes and statements may support a claim; they do not activate a fee profile.

Passwords, PINs, OTPs, credentials, tokens, session cookies, full bank-account numbers, and unnecessary identity numbers are rejected or must be redacted before intake. No broker credentials are stored.
