# Risk-Limit Calibration

Calibration covers synthetic stress, historical research, drawdown, liquidity, fee/slippage, concentration, stale-data, provider-failure, and operator-error scenarios. Each proposed limit reports alternatives, blocked trades, prevented exposure, false-positive blocks, interactions, residual risk, evidence quality, and recommended status.

Calibration output is recommendation-only. Current limits remain unapproved and inactive; no recommendation can approve itself.
