# Campaign Analytics

Daily, five-session weekly, and cumulative reports cover return, DSEX and excess return, drawdown and duration, volatility, Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, profit factor, expectancy, turnover, fees, slippage, exposure, holding period, rejected/missed trades, partial fills, risk interventions, data incidents, and downtime.

Reports separate strategy performance, execution, data quality, risk-engine effects, and operator decisions. All evidence remains campaign scoped.

Short samples make ratios unstable; zero-loss samples may make profit factor undefined or infinite. Reports state that campaigns collect evidence and do not prove profitability.
