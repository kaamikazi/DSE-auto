# Shadow Portfolio Comparison

The comparison engine accepts the reference imported portfolio, buy-and-hold, DSEX, each strategy, and combined paper portfolio. It reports return, drawdown, volatility, Sharpe, Sortino, turnover, fees, slippage, rejected trades, missed trades, and risk interventions.

The imported-session comparison used a short deterministic validation series only. Its ratios are pipeline evidence, not investment evidence; no portfolio is labelled “best” without risk-adjusted robustness review.
