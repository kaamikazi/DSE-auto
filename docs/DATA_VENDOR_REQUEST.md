# DSE Data Vendor Request

Request a written commercial proposal covering:

- licensed DSE quotes with exchange timestamps and provenance semantics;
- historical OHLCV and DSEX values;
- suspensions, trading-status changes, corporate actions, and price-sensitive news;
- market depth, coverage, latency, reconnect behavior, and SLA;
- authentication, rate limits, test environment, retention rights, redistribution limits, support, licensing, and commercial terms.

Ask for schemas, timestamp timezone/precision, correction policy, sample payloads, outage history, and contractually permitted paper-validation use. Certification remains pending until technical and legal review passes. Do not scrape around restrictions, use unofficial endpoints, or disable TLS verification.
