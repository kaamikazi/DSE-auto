# Data-vendor questionnaire

- Which timestamps are exchange events, provider updates, or receipt times?
- Is service real-time or delayed; is Level 1 or Level 2 supplied?
- What history, revision policy, corporate actions, DSEX, market status, and suspension data exist?
- What latency, limits, authentication, SLA, sandbox, support, licensing, and redistribution rights apply?

Answers require documentary evidence before trust changes.
