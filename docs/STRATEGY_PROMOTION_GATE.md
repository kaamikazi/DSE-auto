# Strategy Promotion Gate

`ma_crossover@1.0.0` remains research-only. Readiness checks registration, code/parameter hashes, reviewed dataset, quality/sample size, walk-forward/sensitivity/cost/slippage evidence, strategy risk review, individually approved rules/fees/limits, reviewer decision, and separate operator approval.

Statuses are `not_ready`, `evidence_incomplete`, `review_required`, `ready_for_paper_candidate_review`, and `rejected`. A report never transitions lifecycle state. Missing or conflicting evidence fails closed.
