# Corporate actions

The registry supports cash/stock dividends, bonus shares, rights, splits, reverse splits, symbol changes, mergers, delistings, and suspensions, including announcement, ex, record, and effective dates. Evidence, verification, adjustment factor, affected datasets, and review decision are retained.

Large discontinuities are only `corporate_action_suspected`. Inferred events cannot be automatically verified or approved. Adjusted and unadjusted series must remain explicitly labeled.
