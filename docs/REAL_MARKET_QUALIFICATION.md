# Real-Market Qualification

The target is 60 accepted genuine real-market paper days. The tracker reports planned, completed, reviewed, accepted, rejected, missing, rerun-required, audit-valid, reconciliation-valid, backup-valid, data-quality-passing, unresolved-critical, qualifying, and remaining days.

A day qualifies only when it belongs to a real-market campaign, uses a licensed certified source or explicitly approved operator-attested intake, has trusted provenance, is not synthetic/accelerated, contains every mandatory evidence item, reconciles, has a valid audit and verified backup, passes data quality, and receives an accepted human review.

Milestone 6 synthetic, Milestone 7 emulation, Milestone 9 accelerated infrastructure, fake providers, backtests, and the Milestone 10 five-day workflow dry-run count as zero. Until actual reviewed sessions occur, the authoritative display is `0/60 real-market qualifying days`.
