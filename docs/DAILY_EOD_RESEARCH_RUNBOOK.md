# Daily EOD research runbook

1. Confirm the market is closed.
2. Download approved files manually and record licensing/provenance.
3. Register, hash, and retain the raw files.
4. Review schema and import preview.
5. Run cross-source and corporate-action checks.
6. Attest the source and trading date.
7. Activate clean data for research only.
8. Generate the ma_crossover research output.
9. Simulate read-only portfolio impact and review warnings.
10. Obtain human review.

The software cannot place a real order. Any actual trade is an external manual decision by the account owner.
