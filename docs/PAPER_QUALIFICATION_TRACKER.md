# Paper Qualification Tracker

The tracker targets 60 qualifying DSE trading days. It reports planned, completed, reviewed, accepted, rejected, missing, rerun-required, audit-valid, reconciliation-valid, backup-valid, data-quality-passing, and qualifying days. Remaining qualifying days are displayed directly.

A day qualifies only when it is completed, accepted by a human, audit-valid, reconciled, backed up, and covered by passing data-quality evidence. Qualification fails if any completed day lacks review, any required control is invalid/missing, or a critical incident remains unresolved.

The accelerated Milestone 7 emulation completed 30 days. All were reviewed; one was intentionally rejected. Therefore 29 days qualified and 31 remain. This is workflow evidence only. It is not a substitute for 60 real, representative, independently reviewed DSE sessions.
